Access to the SoundAdam Lab Console is explicitly authorized and controlled. The console is a research service platform — not a publicly open product — so every user must have an active access grant before they can log in or use any of its services. This design ensures that gateway resources, voice experiment infrastructure, and speech processing capacity are allocated to authorized research work and that access can be scoped, audited, and revoked as needed by console administrators and research group leads.

<Warning>
  **Access is not automatically granted.** Submitting a request does not give you immediate access to the Lab Console. All requests go through an authorization review, and access is provisioned only after approval by a console administrator or your research group lead. Plan accordingly when onboarding new team members or starting a new project.
</Warning>

## Access Types

There are two ways access can be granted to the Lab Console: personal access for individual researchers, and group-scoped access for teams operating under a shared research agreement.

### Personal Access

Personal access ties your Lab Console credentials to your individual identity. With personal access, you can:

- Log in to the console at [soundadam.com](https://soundadam.com) with your own credentials
- Create and manage voice experiment sessions under your personal account
- Connect to gateway contexts that have been authorized for your individual access grant
- View and export your own session results and runbook logs

Personal access is appropriate for independent researchers, graduate students running experiments under their own accounts, or collaborators who are joining a project but are not part of the primary research group's group-scoped agreement.

To request personal access, contact the console administrator or the research group lead for the project you are working on. They will initiate the authorization process on your behalf.

### Group-Scoped Access

Group-scoped access provisions credentials and permissions at the research group level. Members of the group share a common access context, which means:

- Group administrators can add and remove members without requiring individual authorization reviews for each person
- Gateway contexts, routing policies, and service configurations can be shared across all group members
- Session results, runbook logs, and experiment data are visible to authorized group members (subject to the group's sharing configuration)
- Access grants and permission changes propagate to all group members simultaneously

Group-scoped access is appropriate for labs, research teams, or project groups that need multiple people working in the same console environment with consistent configuration. The group lead or designated console administrator manages the group's access grant and is responsible for maintaining membership.

## Requesting Access

If you do not yet have access and are not sure which path applies to you:

- **Contact your research group lead or PI** — If you are part of a research group that already uses the Lab Console, your group lead can add you to the existing group-scoped grant. This is the fastest path to access.
- **Contact the console administrator** — If you are an independent researcher or your group does not yet have a Lab Console agreement, reach out to the console administrator at [soundadam.com](https://soundadam.com) to initiate a new access request. The administrator will guide you through the authorization process.

For a full explanation of how authorization is reviewed, what information is required, and what the provisioning timeline looks like, see [Authorization Process](/access/authorization).
