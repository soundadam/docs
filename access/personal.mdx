## What Is Personal Access?

Personal access is an individually scoped authorization mode for the SoundAdam Lab Console. When you operate under personal access, you receive a dedicated gateway context that is fully isolated to your user account — no other researchers share your session state, runbook logs, or voice experiment environment.

This mode is designed for researchers who need a controlled, private workspace within the Lab Console, whether for exploratory experimentation, individual benchmarking, or private testing of new voice pipelines before sharing them with a group.

---

## What Personal Access Isolates

When you activate personal access, the following resources are scoped exclusively to your account:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gateway Context" icon="circle-nodes">
    Your gateway session state is private. No other user can read, modify, or share your gateway context. Each session you start initializes against your personal context only.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Runbook Logs" icon="scroll">
    All session events, interaction traces, and experimental logs are written exclusively to your personal runbook. Other users — including group members — cannot see your entries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voice Experiments" icon="microphone">
    Real-time voice experiments, duplex voice interactions, keyword spotting (KWS) runs, and speech enhancement tasks are all executed within your isolated session boundary.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Session Tokens" icon="key">
    Authorization tokens issued under personal access are scoped to your user identity. They cannot be used to access group-scoped resources without an explicit context switch.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## When to Use Personal Access

Personal access is the right choice when:

- **You are running solo experiments** — benchmarking a KWS model, testing a new speech enhancement pipeline, or validating real-time duplex behavior independently of your team.
- **You need private testing** — you are iterating on a configuration or runbook setup that you do not want visible to your research group until it is ready.
- **You want an isolated baseline** — you need results that are not influenced by simultaneous group-session activity, shared gateway load, or other members' experiment events.
- **You are onboarding to the console** — getting familiar with Lab Console features before participating in a shared group session.

## How to Activate Personal Access

Once you have been authorized by an admin, activating personal access is straightforward:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the Lab Console">
    Navigate to [https://soundadam.com](https://soundadam.com) and sign in with your authorized credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Context Selector">
    After logging in, locate the **Context Selector** in the top navigation bar or the session setup panel. This control shows your currently active session scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Personal Context">
    From the context dropdown, choose **Personal**. The console will confirm the active context by displaying your username or personal context label in the session header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Your Session">
    With personal context active, start a gateway session or launch a voice experiment. All activity from this point will be logged to your personal runbook and remain isolated to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personal Access vs. Group-Scoped Access

| Feature | Personal Access | Group-Scoped Access |
|---|---|---|
| Gateway context | Isolated to your account | Shared across group members |
| Runbook visibility | Private — your logs only | Shared — all members' events visible |
| Voice experiments | Run in isolation | May reflect shared session state |
| Session tokens | Scoped to your user identity | Scoped to the group |
| Best for | Solo work, private testing | Collaborative research, joint experiments |

Switching between personal and group-scoped context can be done at any time from the Context Selector, provided you hold the appropriate authorization for both scopes.

---

<Note>
  Personal access is not self-serve. You must be explicitly authorized by an admin or research group lead before you can log in and activate a personal context. If you have not yet received authorization, see the [Authorization](/access/authorization) page for instructions on how to request access.
</Note>
